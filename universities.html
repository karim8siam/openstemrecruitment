PROPOSED OPERATING MODEL

Proposed process, subject to your approval.

This is a proposal for future partner discussions, not a description of an existing signed recruitment network. Every stage must be adapted to institutional requirements.

INSTITUTIONAL FAQ

Questions institutions may want answered.

Details of recruitment arrangements should be confirmed in writing with each institution.

Is OpenSTEM already an authorized agent for my university?+

No such authorization is asserted by this website. OpenSTEM is inviting exploratory conversations. Representation would require your institution's express approval and appropriate written terms.

What student market would OpenSTEM initially focus on?+

Bangladesh is the intended initial market, with a STEM-oriented academic focus. Expansion or other disciplines would depend on readiness and each institution's requirements.

Are admissions or enrolment volumes guaranteed?+

No. We do not advertise applicant volumes, conversion rates, historic enrolments or student placement figures without substantiated operational data.

Can recruitment begin before a written agreement?+

We would not present ourselves as your authorized agent or submit recruitment referrals under an agency relationship without completing the necessary approvals and documentation.

How would remuneration work?+

Where commissions are legal and permitted, any fees, triggering conditions, payment terms and disclosures should be defined by the signed agreement. No particular commission rate is claimed here.
