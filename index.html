FOR UNIVERSITY TEAMS

A structured approach to student recruitment.

We are seeking prospective institutional relationships, not presenting ourselves as an appointed agent. Cooperation would begin only after verification, institutional approval and written terms.

  • Program eligibility and target-market alignment
  • Applicant consent and accurate information
  • Agreed attribution, reporting and remuneration terms

WHAT YOU CAN EXPECT

Honest information. Clear boundaries.

We believe a recruitment website should be specific about what is known, what is proposed and who makes the final decision.

✓

No invented partnerships.

We will not present an institution as a partner without appropriate written authorization.

✓

No outcome promises.

Admission, funding, scholarships and immigration decisions belong to the relevant decision-makers.

✓

No hidden information transfer.

An enquiry does not authorize sharing personal details with universities or third parties for recruitment.
