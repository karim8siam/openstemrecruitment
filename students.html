STUDENT FAQ

Questions worth asking early.

We want the important limitations to be visible before you share personal information.

Does OpenSTEM guarantee admission or scholarships?+

No. Institutions and funding bodies make their own decisions. We cannot guarantee any acceptance, funding award or faculty response.

Can I apply directly without OpenSTEM?+

Yes. You can apply directly to universities or scholarship providers through their official channels. Our involvement is optional.

Do you have university partners already?+

OpenSTEM is exploring institutional relationships. We do not list any appointed university partner here because no relationship has been independently established for publication.

Can you give me visa advice?+

We can point you to official requirements and checklists. We do not claim to provide regulated immigration advice or visa representation.

How much does guidance cost?+

Sending an initial enquiry costs nothing. If a specific paid service is proposed, its scope and price should be agreed with you in advance; no standard price is advertised here.
